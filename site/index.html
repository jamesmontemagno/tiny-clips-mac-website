Install with Homebrew

Perfect for terminal-first workflows and reproducible setup.

  1. Tap the Tiny Clips cask:
    brew tap jamesmontemagno/tiny-clips https://github.com/jamesmontemagno/tiny-clips
  2. Trust the tap if Homebrew requires it:
    brew trust jamesmontemagno/tiny-clips 2>/dev/null || true
  3. Install Tiny Clips:
    brew install --cask tiny-clips

Optional verify step: brew list --cask tiny-clips

Configurable for your workflow

Tiny Clips includes practical settings for creators and developers: choose save locations, copy captures to clipboard, reveal files in Finder or Explorer, tweak formats, quality, frame rates, webcam overlays, countdowns, and trimmer behavior.

  • Save destinations: Use sensible defaults or choose a custom folder.
  • Clipboard flow: Copy captures automatically after save.
  • Finder/Explorer handoff: Reveal files immediately when export finishes.
  • Screenshot controls: Format, quality, and scaling options.
  • Video controls: 24/30/60 fps plus system and microphone audio toggles.
  • GIF controls: Tune frame rate and max width for lightweight sharing.
  • Webcam overlay: Pick camera, shape, corner, size, and rounded-corner options.
  • Editing flow: Open screenshot editor or trimmer after capture.
  • Countdowns: Optional timer before video and GIF recording starts.
