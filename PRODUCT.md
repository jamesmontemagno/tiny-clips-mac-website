# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Tiny Clips is for Mac and Windows users who need to capture, edit, and share screen content quickly: creators, developers, support teams, educators, and independent makers who move between screenshots, short videos, and GIFs during everyday work. They arrive at the site looking for confidence that the app is lightweight, native-feeling, easy to install, and capable enough for real workflows without becoming another heavy capture suite.

## Product Purpose

The website exists to help visitors understand Tiny Clips, trust it, and download the right version for their platform. Success means a visitor can quickly see what Tiny Clips captures, verify that it fits their Mac or Windows workflow, compare install paths, and leave with the app installed or bookmarked for later.

## Brand Personality

Fast, native, creator-friendly. The voice should feel practical and polished: clear enough for first-time users, specific enough for technical users, and independent enough to avoid corporate software sameness.

## Anti-references

Avoid bloated enterprise SaaS marketing: vague productivity claims, inflated metrics, endless feature-card grids, generic AI landing-page polish, and visual treatments that make a tiny native utility feel like a subscription platform. Also avoid over-technical developer-tool costume; terminal install options should feel useful, not exclusionary.

## Design Principles

- Prove lightweight with concrete product details instead of broad claims.
- Show the app doing real capture work through screenshots, video, and platform-specific install flows.
- Keep platform choice clear, fast, and respectful of Mac and Windows conventions.
- Make every section earn its place; the site should feel as lean as the app.
- Balance creator friendliness with technical trust, especially around installs and permissions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Future design work should preserve strong color contrast, keyboard-accessible tabs and media controls, visible focus states, useful alt text for product imagery, and reduced-motion alternatives for any animation or transition.
